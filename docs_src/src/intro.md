# Introduction

Wrath Access is a screen-reader accessibility mod for **Pathfinder: Wrath of the Righteous**, for
blind and visually-impaired players. It speaks the game's menus, character creation, dialogue,
exploration, combat, and the crusade, and adds custom keyboard navigation over the game's UI,
spatial audio for the world around you, and review "buffers" for inspecting characters in detail.

> **Status: alpha.** A great deal works, but expect rough edges and gaps. Please report bugs (see
> the links below) — that's what the alpha is for. Only PC is supported; console mode (controller
> support) is not implemented.

## What works

- **Speech** through your screen reader (NVDA, JAWS, etc.) or a Windows voice (SAPI 5, OneCore,
  SAPI 4), picked from a list of what's installed.
- **Custom keyboard navigation** in mouse mode, with key-repeat matching your OS settings.
- **Main menu**, the **New Game** flow, and **character creation / level-up**.
- **Settings** (checkboxes, sliders, dropdowns) and **rebindable key bindings**.
- A **mod menu** (Ctrl+M) with the mod's own settings and a **first-run setup wizard** that walks
  you through speech and event feedback.
- **Exploration**: a movement cursor, a categorized **scanner** of everything in the area, a
  **sonar** and other spatial-audio overlays, **wall tones**, room/area awareness with hand-written
  room descriptions, and bookmarks.
- The **world map**: road-by-road travel on the arrow keys, the location list, and the crusade —
  armies, recruiting, resources, and **crusade battles** on their grid.
- **Dialogue**, book events, tutorial popups, and the in-game log / barks.
- **Turn-based combat** and **targeting** (abilities, the action-bar number keys, resting).
- **Service windows**: inventory, character sheet, spellbook, journal, encyclopedia, local map.
- **Vendors / trade**, looting, resting, party selection, formations, and the group manager.
- A **follow-cursor camera** for players with some vision.
- **Review buffers** (Alt+arrows) for reading a unit's details line by line — name, HP, AC, and
  every buff / debuff / condition.
